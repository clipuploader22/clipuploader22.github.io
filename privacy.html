Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Clip Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its operator to upload and schedule videos to YouTube channels that the operator owns or manages.

What data the app accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests two YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos and set their title and scheduled publish time.
  • youtube.readonly – to read the channel's name and ID so the right channel is used, and to check the status of uploaded videos.

The app does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

The access token Google returns is stored only on the operator's own computer and is used only to upload and check videos on that channel. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

A channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that the app can no longer upload to the channel.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
